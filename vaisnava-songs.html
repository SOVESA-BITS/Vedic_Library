Jaya Rādhā Mādhava

jaya rādhā-mādhava kuñja-bihārī
gopī-jana-vallabha giri-vara-dhārī
yaśodā-nandana braja-jana-rañjana
yāmuna-tīra-vana-cārī

Krishna is the lover of Rādhā. He displays many amorous pastimes in the groves of Vrindavana, He is the lover of the cowherd maidens of Vraja, the holder of the great hill named Govardhana, the beloved son of mother Yaśodā, the delighter of the inhabitants of Vraja, and He wanders in the forests along the banks of the River Yamunā.[9][11]

Jaya Rādhe Jaya Kṛṣṇa Jaya Vṛndāvana

jaya rādhe, jaya kṛṣṇa, jaya vṛndāvana
śrī govinda, gopīnātha, madana-mohana

All glories to Rādhā, Kṛṣṇa, and Vṛndāvana! All glories to Govinda, Gopīnātha, and Madana-mohana!

Gaura Pahu Na Bhajiya

(1) gaura pahu na bhajiya mainu
prema-ratana-dhana helaya harainu
(2) adhane jatana kori dhana teyaginu
apana karama-dose apani dubinu
(3) sat-sanga chadi’ kainu asate vilasa
te-karane lagilo ye karma-bandha-phansa
(4) visaya-visama-visa satata khainu
gaura-kirtana-rase maghana na hainu
(5) keno va achaye prana ki sukha paiya
narottam das keno na gelo mariya

I did not worship Lord Gaurāṅga, and thus I have lost the priceless jewel of divine love... [full translation in source][2]

Parama Karuṇā

parama karuṇā, pahu dui jana
nitāi gauracandra
saba avatāra-sāra śiromaṇi
kevala ānanda-kanda

bhajo bhajo bhāi, caitanya nitāi
sudṛḍha biśwāsa kori
viṣaya chāḍiyā, se rase majiyā
mukhe bolo hari hari

dekho ore bhāi, tri-bhuvane nāi
emona doyāla dātā
paśu pākhī jhure, pāṣāṇa vidare
śuni jāńra guṇa-gāthā

saṁsāri māgiyā, kāńdiyā kāńdiyā
prabhu nā pāya bāro
emona doyāla, dataa pāi nā
śuni jāńra guṇa-gāthā

Lord Nityānanda and Lord Gaurāṅga are supremely merciful, the essence and crest-jewel of all incarnations, and the source of all bliss. [Full lyrics truncated for brevity, see source for more.]

Gaurāṅga Bolite Habe

(1) gaurāṅga bolite habe pulaka-śarīra
hari hari bolite nayane ba’be nīra
(2) āra kabe nitāi-cānder koruṇā hoibe
saṁsāra-vāsanā mora kabe tuccha ha’be

When will my body thrill with ecstasy upon chanting "Gaurāṅga"? When will my eyes fill with tears upon chanting "Hari Hari"? [3][10][12]

Gaurāṅgera Duti Pada

gaurāṅgera duti pada, jāra dhana sampada
se jāne bhakati-rasa-sāra
gaurāṅgera madhura-līlā, jāra karṇe praveśilā
hṛdaya nirmala bhelo tāra

One who considers the two lotus feet of Lord Gaurāṅga as his only treasure knows the true essence of devotional service.

Nava Gaura Varam

(1) nava gaura-varam nava-puṣpa-śaram
nava-bhāva-dharam nava-lāsya-param
nava-hāsya-karam nava-hema-varam
praṇamāmi śacī-suta-gaura-varam
(2) nava-prema-yutam nava-nīta-śucam
nava-veśa-kṛtam nava-prema-rasam
navadhā vilasat śubha-prema-mayam
praṇamāmi śacī-suta-gaura-varam

I worship the ever-fresh Lord Gaurāṅga, adorned with a new garland of flowers, residing in a new temple on the bank of the river, dressed in new yellow cloth, with a complexion like a fresh raincloud, and a beautiful form like the new moon.[4]

Mana Re Kaha Na Gaura

(1) mana re! kaha nā gaura kathā
gaurera nāma, amiyāra dhāma, pirīti mūrati dātā
(2) śayane gaura, svapane gaura, gaura nayanera tārā
jīvane gaura, maraṇe gaura, gaura galāra hārā

O mind, chant the name of Lord Gaura! By crossing the ocean of material existence, you will attain deliverance.[5]

Bhaja Gaurāṅga

(refrain) bhaja gaurāṅga kaha gaurāṅga laha gaurāṅgera nāma re
je jan gaurāṅga bhaje, sei āmāra prāṇa re
(1) gaurāṅga boliyā du’ bāhu tuliyā nāciyā nāciyā beḍāo re
(2) gaurāṅga bhajile gaurāṅga japile hoy duḥkhera abasāna re

Worship Lord Gaurāṅga! Chant Gaurāṅga! Speak about Lord Gaurāṅga only! Those who worship Lord Gaurāṅga are indeed my life and soul.[6]

Gaurāṅga Karuṇā Karo

(1) gaurāṅga karuṇā koro dīna hīna jane
mosamo patita prabhu nāhi tribhūvane
(2) dante tṛṇa dhari gaura ḍāki he tomāre
kṛpā kari eso āmār hṛdaya mandire
(3) jadi dayā nā karibe patita dekhiyā
patita pāvana nāma kisera lāgiyā
(4) paḍechi bhava tuphāne nāhika nistāra
śrī caraṇa taraṇī dāne dāse kara pāra
(5) śrī kṛṣṇa caitanya prabhu dāser anudāsa
prārthanā karaye sadā narottama dāsa

O my dear Lord Gauranga! Please show Your mercy to this lowly and destitute soul. O Lord! There is no one more fallen than myself in all the three worlds.[7]

Kiba Jaya Jaya Gaura

kiba jaya jaya gaura-cānda
kiba jaya jaya nityānanda

All glories to Lord Gaura Cānda! All glories to Lord Nityānanda!

Yadi Gaura Na Hoito

yadi gaura na hoito tabe ki hoito
kemone dharitām de
rādhāra mahimā, prema-rasa-sīmā
jagate jānāta ke?

If Lord Gaura had not appeared, what would have become of us? Who would have revealed the glories of Rādhā and the limits of divine love?

Jagannātha Aṣṭakam

kadācit kālindī-taṭa-vipina-saṅgīta-kavaro
mudābhīrī-nārī-vadana-kamalāsvāda-madhupaḥ
ramā-śambhu-brahmāmara-pati-gaṇeśārcita-pado
jagannāthaḥ svāmī nayana-pathagāmī bhavatu me
[...full 8 verses available on request...]

May Lord Jagannātha, whose lotus feet are worshiped by Lakṣmī, Śiva, Brahmā, Indra, and Gaṇeśa, and who is the delight of the gopīs on the banks of the Yamunā, always reside in the vision of my eyes.

Jaya Jaya Jagannātha

jaya jaya jagannātha, jaya balabhadra
jaya subhadrā, jaya sudarśana

All glories to Lord Jagannātha, Balabhadra, Subhadrā, and Sudarśana!

śuddha-bhakata-caraṇa-reṇu, bhajana-anukūla
bhakata-sevā, parama-siddhi, prema-latikāra mūla

The dust of the feet of pure devotees is favorable for devotional service. Serving the devotees is the highest perfection and the root of the creeper of divine love.
